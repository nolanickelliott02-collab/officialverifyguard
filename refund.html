Refund Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

VerifyGuard is a product of Verify Guard Apps LLC (Louisiana). The app is free to start; paid plans (Basic, Pro, Guardian) are monthly subscriptions sold and billed by Apple through the App Store.

Requesting a refund

Because Apple processes every VerifyGuard payment, Apple — not us — decides and issues refunds. We can't see your card details or reverse a charge ourselves. To ask for one:

  • Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account, or
  • Follow Apple's guide: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

Apple reviews each request under its own policies and laws where you live.

Canceling

You can cancel anytime in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or see Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. After you cancel, you keep your plan until the end of the period you already paid for. We don't give partial refunds for unused time, except where the law or Apple's policies require it.

Promo codes and free periods

If you start a plan with a promo code (an Apple offer code), Apple shows the price, the length of the offer, and when regular billing starts before you confirm. If you don't want to be charged, cancel before the offer ends.

Need help?

If something went wrong with a scan or a feature you paid for, email verifyguardsupport@gmail.com and we'll help — including explaining how to ask Apple for a refund.
