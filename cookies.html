Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

officialverifyguard.com is run by Verify Guard Apps LLC (Louisiana).

We don't use cookies

This website doesn't set any cookies — not for analytics, advertising, or anything else — and doesn't use similar technologies such as tracking pixels, browser fingerprinting, or local storage. Because nothing is stored on your device, there's no cookie banner to accept.

No third-party content

The site doesn't embed videos, social media widgets, ads, or analytics scripts, and its fonts are served from this site itself. Visiting it doesn't send information to Google, Meta, or any other third party. Links to other sites (like the App Store or Scampedia) only take you there if you click them, and those sites have their own policies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure its service; we don't receive or use them.

The VerifyGuard app

The iOS app doesn’t use cookies for tracking or advertising. What the app collects is described in our Privacy Policy.

Changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
